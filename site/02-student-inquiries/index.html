Ep 02 — Migration & Education Agency: 5 loops so no student goes unanswered

Watch: Automating Student Inquiries With AI Workflows (13 min)

This is the plan I gave the agency — an advisory walkthrough, not a deployed build. The numbers below are my estimates from the video, explained there.

The problem

  • Student inquiries arrive as DMs at all hours, in more than one language
  • High volume means messages get missed or answered late
  • The website is out of date, has no online booking, and no API to connect to
  • Staff are the bottleneck for every single reply

The 5 daily loops

  1. Bilingual inquiry responder — AI drafts replies to every inquiry in the student's language, using the agency's own course and visa information. Staff review and approve before sending.
  2. Policy watchdog — keeps an eye on migration/policy changes and flags anything that affects the advice students are getting, so answers never go stale.
  3. Document checklist chaser — scans every 30 minutes for applications with missing documents and chases the student with a friendly, specific reminder ("we still need your English test result").
  4. Lifecycle nurture — scans the whole student list and picks up multi-year follow-ups (e.g. students who will need the next visa step in 6–12 months) so no past client is forgotten.
  5. Consult booking + reminders — books consultations and sends reminders so students actually show up, without staff playing calendar tag.

Estimated impact (my numbers, from the video)

  • ~30% efficiency improvement across inquiry handling
  • Roughly 3–6 weeks to implement, working around the existing website (no API needed — the workflows meet the business where it is)

What a human still does

  • Approves replies before they go out
  • Owns all actual migration advice — the AI drafts admin answers, it never advises
  • Handles anything the loops flag as unusual
