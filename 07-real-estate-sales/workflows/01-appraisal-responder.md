# Appraisal request responder

## What it does

The enquiry that starts every listing — answered the same day, even on weekends. The AI replies from your agency's process, books the appraisal visit into your calendar, and sends the seller a confirmation. You just show up.

## The loop

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  A[Seller enquiry Sunday] --> B[AI replies with your process]
  B --> C[Appraisal visit booked]
  C --> D[Confirmation + prep list to seller]
  D --> E[You attend the visit]
```

## What a human still does

- Approves the reply and the booking window
- Handles anything about price expectations

## What it runs on

Watches the website enquiry form + SMS. Books into your real calendar.
